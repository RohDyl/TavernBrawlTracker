🍺 The Table Is the Terrain

No terrain pieces needed. Objects on the table count as Clutter (Scavenge) and Cover (+4 Defence). Make each piece of Clutter at least 1" across, so "touching Clutter" is fair. Models move around Clutter taller than 1" and may climb over lower Clutter freely.

Mark each activation by placing the spent Order Token beside the model. Several Scrappers share one reference card, and a Brute may activate twice, so don't rely on rotating a card. Clear those tokens in the Order Phase.

⏱ Game Duration

The game lasts 4 turns or until one squad is fully KO'd.

📖 The Three Stats

Every model has Skill (how well it hits), Blood (its health), and Defence (how well it blocks).

🪨 Brute — Heavy Hitter

A Brute generates 2 Order Tokens in the Order Phase instead of 1, and may be activated twice per turn (one Order Token each). A Command Token may still activate it a third time.

🛡 Thick-Skinned. A Brute always rolls +1 defence die against any attack that lands at least one die (this stacks with Stand Your Ground, cards and Dig In). This counts as a bonus defence die, so Dart stops it.

There is no activation cap. Command Tokens stack on top of those Order Tokens — with the Colonel's Tactical Genius a Brute can activate four times. Worth watching.

⭐ Command Token

Your BB generates one Command Token per turn. Losing your BB means losing this for the rest of the game. Command Tokens are separate from Order Tokens.

03

Setting Up

1. Table: any table about 36" × 30" (90 × 75 cm) is ideal. Anything 30–36" deep plays well; avoid tables deeper than 36", where Turn 1 has little action. Objects on it become terrain.
2. Each player takes one edge. Deploy within 6" of your edge. That strip is your deployment zone.
3. Place the 3 Prize Tokens along the centre line, at least 6" apart and at least 6" from each deployment zone (so at least 12" from each table edge).
4. Shuffle the Gear deck. Each player draws an opening hand of 5 cards.
5. Both roll a d20. Highest wins Turn 1 initiative. Reroll ties.
6. Alternate placing models one at a time. Initiative player places first.
🩸 Starting Blood
  • Scrapper — 4
  • Brute — 7
  • Big Bozza — see card
💀 KO'd

A model at 0 blood tokens is immediately removed from the table.

✋ Opening Hand

Both players draw a flat 5 cards regardless of squad size — leaner crews get a fuller start.

04

Core Concepts

👊 Engaged

Within 1" of an enemy model, measured base edge to base edge (for meeples, from the edge of the meeple's footprint). All distances are measured the same way. Affects Retaliation and Fist Fights. A model may be engaged with more than one enemy.

👁 Line of Sight

Trace a straight line attacker to target. Blocked by models and terrain taller than the model.

🛡 Cover

A model is in Cover if it is within 1" of a piece of Clutter that lies between it and the attacker (a line from attacker to defender crosses the Clutter). In Cover, its Defence value is +4. Cover applies to Fist Fights and ranged attacks. The same object counts as Clutter for Scavenge.

📖 Glossary
Turn — one full round (Order, Action and End Phase) shared by both players. The game lasts 4 turns.
Activation — one model taking its option (Move & Act, Stand Your Ground, Leg It or Shove Through). "This turn" means until the End Phase of the current turn.
Action — the one thing you do after moving: a Fist Fight, one Order card, or a BB Active. Leg It and Shove Through have no Action.
Free / Buff — one Free Action card per activation, played on top of the Action. It does not cost an Order Token.
Reaction — played in response to the trigger printed on the card (or, if none, a declared action). No Order cost.
On a hit — the attack deals at least 1 damage after defence.
Free Fist Fight — a Fist Fight that costs no Order Token and no Action (Retaliation, reactions, Running Start, Mother Malone, and so on). It rolls the model's Atk dice plus passive bonuses only: no Gang Up, no Stand Your Ground, no buff cards, no Come On Then!. The defender still defends normally, including Cover and Thick-Skinned.
Bonus dice — extra dice from cards, Stand Your Ground, Dig In and Thick-Skinned. Dart stops bonus defence dice.
Push — move the model directly away from the source the stated distance. It stops early at other models, terrain taller than 1" and the table edge. Pushing never provokes Retaliation. Big Sal can't be pushed.
Clutter — an object on the table, at least 1" across. Touching it lets you Scavenge. Within 1" of it, with it between you and the attacker, is Cover.
05

Turn Structure

🏆 What you're playing for

KO the whole enemy squad and you win immediately. Otherwise the game ends after Turn 4 and the most points wins: a Prize is 3, the enemy BB is 3, a Brute is 2, a Scrapper is 1. Tiebreakers and the full claim rules are in Winning.

1
Order Phase
Clear every spent Order Token from beside models. Activations reset.
Gain 1 Order Token per model on the table, including BB (Brutes: 2).
Place a Command Token coin beside your BB's card (if alive).
Draw 1 Gear card per friendly model on the table (incl. BB).
Bid for initiative (Turn 2 onwards).
💰 Initiative — Tip the Barman (Turn 2+)

Each player may bid one card face down (or bid nothing). Reveal together. Tip values rank 10c < 20c < 50c < R1 < R2. Highest tip wins; if only one player bids, they win. All bid cards are discarded.

No bids: the current holder keeps initiative. Tie: the player who did NOT have initiative last turn wins.

Order Tokens are a shared pool

Gain 1 Order Token per model on the table, including BB (Brutes give 2). The tokens sit in one pool, but each model may be activated by only one Order Token per turn — a Brute by up to two (Heavy Hitter). Command Tokens are separate. A card that says otherwise (Daily Special) can still activate a model that has already used its Order Tokens.

Drawing & Hand Limit

Your card income scales with your surviving crew — draw 1 per model each Order Phase. Wide squads run a card engine; lean squads rely on big swings and Scavenge.

Hand limit: 7. You may hold more than 7 cards until the End Phase, then discard down to 7.

When the Gear deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck. If both are empty, no more cards are drawn.

Command Token

Generated by your BB. Spending a Command Token is your activation for that slot in the alternation. You may spend it whenever it is your turn to activate, even if you still have Order Tokens. It may activate any friendly model — even one already activated this turn.

Each model may only benefit from one Command Token activation per turn, unless a specific ability states otherwise. If your BB is KO'd, no Command Token is generated for the rest of the game. There is no separate cap on total activations.

2
Action Phase

The initiative player decides who activates first. Players alternate. Spend 1 Order Token (or a Command Token) → choose a legal model → place that spent Order Token beside the model → take the activation (see Section 06). A Brute may be chosen twice, once per Order Token.

Passing: No tokens or choose not to activate. Phase ends when both pass consecutively.

3
End Phase
1. Claim Prize Tokens. Any unengaged model within 1" of a Prize Token claims it — score 3 points and remove the token. Measure to the edge of the token. One model may claim more than one token. All claims are simultaneous. Engaged models never claim. If both players have an unengaged model within 1" of the same Prize Token, it is contested: nobody claims it and it stays on the table.
2. If this is Turn 4, the game ends — score it (see Winning). Wipe-outs are checked immediately at any time, not only here.
3. Discard down to 7 cards.
06

Actions

Activation sequence

1) Spend an Order Token (or Command Token) and choose the model and option (Move & Act / Stand Your Ground / Leg It / Shove Through). 2) Move. 3) Take the Action (Fist Fight, Order card or BB Active). You may play your one buff/Free card at any point during steps 2–3, and Desperate Acts at their printed timing. 4) Scavenge if touching Clutter. Reactions may interrupt at any declared step.

Leg It and Shove Through skip step 3 — no Action this activation. You may still play a buff/Free card and Desperate Acts on Leg It.

⚡ Your Activation

Spend 1 Order Token to activate a model (a Brute may do this twice per turn). On Move & Act it may Move up to its Speed AND take ONE Action, and may play ONE buff / Free Action card on top.

Your Action is ONE of:

  • Fist Fight an engaged enemy, or
  • play one Order card (e.g. a ranged attack or heal), or
  • use your BB's Active ability.

The Move and the Action are separate — on Move & Act you always get to move and take an Action. You simply cannot Fist Fight and play an Order card in the same activation; they share the one Action slot. Reactions and Desperate Acts are separate and may still be played at their proper timing (a Desperate Act may stack on top of your one buff card — that's your Hail-Mary lane).

Your Options — Pick One

⚔️
Move & Act
The default. Move up to Speed, then Fist Fight an engaged enemy OR play 1 Order card (or use a BB ability).
⚠️ Provokes Retaliation if you start within 1" and the move takes you out of 1".
🥊
Stand Your Ground
No movement → Fist Fight. Scrapper: +1 attack die. Brute: +1 defence die until this Brute's next activation starts (with Heavy Hitter that may be later this turn).
💨
Leg It
Move up to Speed twice. Each move may go a different direction. No Action this activation; you may still play a buff/Free card and Desperate Acts.
⚠️ Provokes Retaliation on each move that leaves 1".
🚶
Shove Through
Move up to Speed in a straight line. The path can't cross a model or terrain taller than 1". No Action this activation.
✦ Does not provoke Retaliation, even if the model started engaged. Only Greasy Floor stops it.
🎴 Scavenge — a Free Action

Each model may Scavenge once per turn. A model touching Clutter draws 2 Gear cards, keeps 1, and discards the rest. It does not use your Action — fighting in the junk pays off.

07

Combat

Must be engaged (within 1") to make a Fist Fight — including a free Fist Fight. One rule governs every die, attack or defence: roll exactly the value = Crit, roll a natural 1 = automatic fail.

⚔️🛡 Combat Resolution

Step 1 — Attack. Roll your attack dice. Each die:

  • Exactly your Skill → CRIT: deals 2 damage; can only be stopped by a Crit Block.
  • 2 up to your Skill → Hit: deals 1 damage.
  • Natural 1 → Miss.

Defensive Reactions (Pocket Sand, Trash Can Lid, Dig In, Whoops!) are played after the attack dice are rolled and before any defence dice are rolled. For Pocket Sand, the defender chooses which attack die is removed.

Step 2 — Defence. Roll one defence die per landed attack die (Hit or Crit), plus any bonus dice from cards, stance or Thick-Skinned. Each die:

  • Exactly your Defence → CRIT BLOCK: stops 2 damage — it can cancel a Crit, or mop up two Hits.
  • 2 up to your Defence → Block: stops 1 Hit. (A normal Block cannot touch a Crit.)
  • Natural 1 → Fail.

Step 3 — Resolve. Assign blocks to the defender's advantage. Net damage = everything left unblocked. You can never block more damage than was dealt. The defender loses that much Blood.

🎯 Worked Example

Brute (Skill 14, 3 dice) rolls 14, 9, 1 → one Crit (2 dmg) + one Hit (1 dmg) + a miss = 3 raw, 2 landed dice. The defender may now play Pocket Sand, Trash Can Lid, Dig In or Whoops! before defence dice.

Scrapper (Def 10) rolls 2 defence dice: 10, 6 → a Crit Block + a Block. The Crit Block cancels the Crit; the Block stops the Hit. Net: 0 — a perfect parry!

If the defender had been a Brute, Thick-Skinned would add a third defence die, because at least one attack die landed.

👥 Gang Up

Scrappers roll +1 attack die per friendly Scrapper within 4", max +2. Applies only to a model's primary Fist Fight — never to free, extra, or Reaction Fist Fights.

Retaliation

When an engaged model starts a move within 1" of an enemy and that move takes it out of 1", or plays a ranged card while engaged, every enemy it was engaged with makes a free Fist Fight first.

  • Does not cost Orders or an Action. Resolves before the provoking action.
  • If the provoker is KO'd, their action is cancelled.
  • If the provoker is KO'd mid-Retaliation, all remaining retaliations from that trigger cancel.
  • Retaliation cannot trigger further Retaliation. Gang Up does not apply (it's a free Fist Fight).

Ranged Attacks

Only via Gear cards. Require Line of Sight. They roll only the dice printed on the card.

  • You may not ranged-attack an enemy you are engaged with — throw fists, not bottles.
  • You may target a different, unengaged enemy, but this provokes Retaliation from everyone engaging you.

Push

Push: move the model directly away from the source the stated distance. It stops early at other models, terrain taller than 1" and the table edge. Pushing never provokes Retaliation. Big Sal can't be pushed.

🍺 A few rounds at the bar
  • A full activation. A Scrapper starts 3" from an enemy, moves into 1", plays Broken Bottle, and Fist Fights. No Retaliation — it did not start that move within 1".
  • Gang Up. Three friendly Scrappers, two of them within 4" of the one fighting: +2 dice, which is the cap. The third is 5" away and adds nothing. A free Fist Fight would get none of them.
  • Retaliation chain. A Brute starts within 1" of two Scrappers and Legs It out. Both make a free Fist Fight first. If the first KO's the Brute, the second is cancelled and Leg It never happens.
  • A Brute's two goes. Spend one Order Token, place it beside the Brute, and fight. Later, spend the second and activate it again. Stand Your Ground's extra defence die ends when that second activation starts. A Command Token can still wake it a third time.
  • Prize or a standoff. Your unengaged Scrapper and their unengaged Brute are both within 1" of a Prize: contested, nobody claims it. If only your Scrapper is there, you claim it — and one model may claim two tokens in the same End Phase.

Losing vs. Spending Blood

Losing = damage from attacks or cards. Spending = using a Desperate Act. Both reduce your count. At 0 — KO'd, removed from table.

If several effects would save a model from KO (Last Stand, Won't Stay Down), its owner picks one; the others are not used up.

🩸 Becoming Bloodied

Big Bozzas: Flip card to Bloodied side permanently.

Scrappers & Brutes: Track via token count. Bloodied effects apply at half Blood or below.

🔥 Bloodied = Unlock

When Bloodied, your Desperate Acts switch to their enhanced versions, and you may perform two Desperate Acts per activation. Getting hurt makes you dangerous.

09

Desperate Acts

Three clean gambles — one for Moving, one for Hitting, one for Blocking. All are paid in blood, sit outside your Action slot, and may stack on top of a buff card.

The Rules of Desperation
  • Cost is paid in blood. Minimum cost 1.
  • A Desperate Act can never reduce you below 1 blood — you cannot KO yourself.
  • Each model may use one Desperate Act per activation (two if Bloodied). This counts Desperate Acts used as Reactions during an enemy's activation.
  • Bloodied isn't a discount; it's an unlock to the enhanced effect.

Gear Cards

Order

Your Action for the activation. One per activation.

Reaction

Played in response to the trigger printed on the card (or, if none, a declared action). No Order cost. If both players react to the same thing, the non-active player plays first, then the active player; each Reaction resolves fully before the next. Free / Buff

One per activation, on top of your Action. Buffs layer onto a Fist Fight.

⭐ Special Action

Each Special Action card defines exactly when it may be played — the timing printed on the card overrides normal play rules. Special Actions do not cost an Order Token unless stated. No archetype bonuses apply to Special Action cards.

Archetype Bonuses

Archetype bonuses require your matching BB to be alive. KO'd BB = bonuses lost for the rest of the game.

Big Bozzas & Archetypes

BB cards are double-sided — when Bloodied (half Blood or below), flip permanently. Each BB's Signature may be used once per turn at the timing printed on it (Reactions fire in the enemy's activation). A card marked Once per game (Won't Stay Down) is used once per game. A Signature never uses the buff slot, is never in the Gear deck, and is never discarded. A BB's Active ability is its Action for that activation (it does not also Fist Fight, unless the ability says so).

⚠️ Self-KO Scoring

Points for KO'ing a model are awarded to the opponent regardless of cause — including BB area effects. (A model can never KO itself by spending blood on a Desperate Act.)

Victory Conditions

⚡ Immediate Win

All enemy models KO'd at any point — you win immediately. If both squads are fully KO'd at the same moment (Right Back Atcha, Ashtray splash, Table Flip), there is no immediate win: score normally, then apply the tiebreakers.

🏆 After 4 Turns

If this is Turn 4, the game ends and the player with most points wins.

Tiebreakers (in order)

  1. Whose BB is still alive
  2. Whose BB has more blood remaining
  3. If still tied — draw
Concurrent Triggers

When two effects would happen at the same time, the player with initiative chooses which resolves first.

Tuning Dials Playtest appendix

Levers for the table, not secret rules. Heavy Hitter and Thick-Skinned are core now. Reach for a dial only if the problem actually shows up, and don't stack several at once.

🛡 Brutes too strong, or too weak? Sim-tested

Thick-Skinned and Heavy Hitter are the live rules. Too strong? Drop Thick-Skinned first. Too weak? Brute Blood 8. Do not use Def 8 — it overshoots.

🥊 Stand Your Ground + DA stacking Untested

By default these stack (a Scrapper can plant for +1 die and Come On Then!). Dial: if that combo feels oppressive, rule that Stand Your Ground's bonus does not stack with a Desperate Act on the same swing.

🛡 Defensive reactions feel too slow? Sim-tested

Live rule: play them after the attack roll, before defence dice. Dial: if defence feels too reactive or slow, declare defensive reactions before the roll. Use this only together with a Brute re-tune — blind reactions shift the 4 Scrapper vs 2 Brute matchup hard.

🃏 Card hoarding (Barfly/Grifter)? Sim-tested

The 7-card End-Phase discard should handle it. Dial: lower the hand limit to 6. Don't cap the Order-Phase draw — that hurts wide squads.

📏 Turn 1 too quiet, or too bloody? Sim-tested

The ideal table is about 36" × 30". Turn 1 too quiet → shrink the depth (30" → 24"). Too bloody → go to 36" deep. Avoid anything deeper than 36".

🛡 Un-killable cover-turtle BB? Parked

Bonus defence dice stay uncapped. Over-investing in defence is its own tax. No sign of an unkillable boss in the v9.2 sims (most games end in a wipe, and every Bozza sits inside 45–55%). Dial, if it ever shows up: cap bonus defence dice at +2 per attack. If you adopt that, give Dig In Bloodied a different unlock so it isn't dead.

🔭 The 3 Things To Watch
  • Do Brutes feel tanky but not oppressive? Four Scrappers against two Brutes should be a coin flip.
  • Colonel + 2 Brutes. There is no activation cap, so Tactical Genius on a double-activating Brute is four goes. Maggie, Barry and Big Sal still like the top of the table.
  • Wipe-outs ended about 72% of sim games. Do prizes still matter at the table, and does anyone ever fire a ranged Order card?
